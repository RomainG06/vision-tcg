🎯 Hunting Concepts & Terminology

All UI labels use hunting metaphors. Every screen communicates the "hunt" narrative.
PROFIL DE CHASSE
Hunt Profile — custom search configurations, objectives, boosts, exclusions
RADAR
Live Detection — animated sweep showing active hunts & detected targets
OPPORTUNITÉS
Detected Listings — feed of matching items ranked by score
NIVEAU DE RARETÉ
Rarity Level — Common → Rare → Epic → Legendary → Mythique
SCORE D'OPPORTUNITÉ
Opportunity Score — 0–100 ranking of listing fit (algorithm-driven)
RISQUE
Risk Assessment — Low / Medium / High risk indicator per listing
POTENTIEL
Value Gain — estimated profit/return on purchase
ALERTE
Notification Trigger — alert conditions & thresholds
WATCHLIST
Saved Items — tracked listings for later action
HISTORIQUE
Hunt History — past hunts, discoveries, actions, conversions

📈 Scoring System (0–100)

Each opportunity receives an AI-calculated score based on boosts, exclusions, and market data.
Score Range Tier Name Icon Interpretation
0–39 Normal ○ Standard listing, no special interest
40–59 Intéressant ◆ Interesting, worth monitoring
60–74 Rare ◈ Good opportunity detected
75–89 Épique ★ Strong opportunity, act soon
90–100 Légendaire ✦ Exceptional, contact immediately
ANOMALY Mythique ✧ Price anomaly, verify urgently
